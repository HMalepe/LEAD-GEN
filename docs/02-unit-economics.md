## 1. The funnel (water base case)

```
 100 raw leads (R60 paid media each + R3 AI/messaging)
  │  50% pass AI qualification
  ▼
  50 qualified
  │  65% book a same-day / next-day visit
  ▼
 32.5 booked
  │  85% are home when the installer arrives
  ▼
 27.6 attended  ──►  billed at R550 each
```

**3.6 raw leads per attended visit → R228 fully loaded cost → R322 contribution margin (59%).**

The funnel rates are higher than solar's because the demand is urgent (dry taps). The **R60 cost per raw lead is the one assumption with no data behind it**: a hyper-local Click-to-WhatsApp audience in a live outage should be cheap, but only the pilot can say.

## 3. What the board should take from this

1. **Paid-media CPL is the existential number.** Above R149 per raw lead, every visit loses money at base conversion. The kill switch is wired into the KPI table ([01](01-business-plan.md) §7) and measurable per ad from day 1 (`ad_performance` view).
2. **Price is the biggest profit lever.** A 20% price cut costs twice what a 20% drop in any funnel stage does. Discount the platform fee, never the per-visit price.
3. **The three funnel rates multiply.** Show rate is the cheapest to defend: reminders cost cents. Same-day slots, the 2h reminder and the attendance check exist for that reason.
4. **The installer's maths must work too.** At R550 per attended visit and a 30% close rate, the installer pays R1,833 per won R16,500 job: 11% of revenue, about 38% of their gross margin. The research's suggested R700 *lead* costs R2,333 per won job *before* no-shows, roughly 49% of gross margin ([06](06-niche-research-review.md) §2.1). We're cheaper *and* we carry the no-show risk.
5. **Thinner per-unit margin than solar, faster cycle.** A water visit earns R322 against R559 for solar, but water closes in days, not months, and repeats (pumps, filtration, upgrades). Cash turns faster, and the pilot reads in weeks.

## 4. Cash flow and working capital

Ad spend leaves our account daily; revenue arrives after the visit and the 48h dispute window. In the base case at month 12 that's **~R78k/month of ad spend ahead of billing**.

The fix is structural, and it's implemented:
- Installers **prepay** (≥ R5,500 = 10 visits) through a Paystack link sent on WhatsApp (`TOPUP`).
- Routing **reserves** credit for every booked-but-unbilled visit, so an installer can never be booked beyond their balance.
- When available credit drops below one week of expected visits, their **ads pause automatically**, and they resume when a top-up lands.
- The balance lives in an append-only `credit_ledger` (`client_balance` view, migration 0004). One charge per visit is enforced by a unique index.
