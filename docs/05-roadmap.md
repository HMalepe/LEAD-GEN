# 05 — Roadmap

> Rule: **ship to real users before it feels ready.** Every phase ends at a measurable gate, not a date.

## Phase 0: Validate (days 1–14) · cost about R10k

| Deliverable | Gate to pass |
|---|---|
| 15 installer interviews (pricing, pain, capacity) | ≥ 3 agree to a pilot at R1,200 per attended appointment |
| Compliance pre-launch checklist ([04](04-compliance.md) §5) | All items ticked |
| WhatsApp Business account verified and templates approved | Live test message delivered |
| Manual "concierge" qualification: the founder runs the WhatsApp chats by hand with a script | Proves the questions convert before anything is automated |

**Why manual first:** automating a script that hasn't been proven only gets you to the wrong answer faster. Run 30 conversations by hand, then encode what works.

## Phase 1: MVP (days 15–45) · cost about R40k (mostly ad spend)

| Week | Build | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | WhatsApp webhook → DB → AI qualification (v0 rules) | Median first response < 60 s |
| 4 | Booking into client calendars plus confirmation | Qualified → booked ≥ 50% |
| 5 | Show-rate engine (24h and 2h reminders, attendance check) | Show rate ≥ 75% |
| 6 | Client dashboard with Won/Lost button, plus Paystack prepaid credit | ≥ 90% outcomes reported |

**Day-60 go/no-go gate (from the business plan):**
cost per attended appointment ≤ R650 · show rate ≥ 75% · ≥ 5 paying clients.
→ **Pass:** scale. **Fail:** fix the weakest funnel stage for 2 weeks, then pivot the vertical or stop.

## Phase 2: Scale (months 3–6)

- Grow to 10–15 clients in Gauteng solar
- Launch vertical #2: **security** (same homeowners, cross-sell with consent)
- First 2 owned SEO sites ("rank and rent")
- Scoring v1 (logistic regression on about 300 outcomes)
- Upload offline conversions to Meta and Google so the ad platforms optimise for won deals
- First hire: **operations and client success** (part-time) to handle disputes, onboarding and QA sampling

## Phase 3: Compound (months 6–12)

- Expand to Cape Town and Durban (new territories, same playbook)
- Territory Lock upsell to the top 20% of clients
- Scoring v2 using transcript intent features
- **Productise:** license the platform to installers with their own ad budgets, as SaaS at R3k–R8k/month. It adds recurring revenue on top of the service margin. *Assumption: price to validate.*
- Target: base-case run-rate of 375 attended appointments a month

## Phase 4: Optionality (12 months+)

| Option | Logic |
|---|---|
| Vertical SaaS | The platform works for any appointment-based local service |
| Financing referrals | Solar finance partners pay for qualified finance applicants (regulated, so only with proper licensing and partnerships) |
| Data-driven installer rating | Outcome data shows which installers actually close and deliver. That could become a consumer-facing marketplace with a trust layer. |
| Exit | Lead-gen businesses with owned SEO assets, recurring retainers and proprietary outcome data are acquirable by marketing groups and the vertical's suppliers |

## Week-1 action list (start Monday)

1. Register the company and open a business bank account (if not done)
2. Register the Information Officer
3. Build the 60-installer list from Google Maps and supplier directories
4. Book 15 interviews: calls and visits, not cold email
5. Set up WhatsApp Business (Meta Business Manager verification takes days, so start now)
6. Write the 5-question qualification script and run it by hand on the first 30 test leads
