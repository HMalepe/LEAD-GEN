# Source research: Home-service lead-gen niches, Sandton / Bryanston / Randburg (Nov 2026)

> **Status:** source material as received on 7 Nov 2026, condensed to its claims and figures. Not edited for correctness.
> The critical review, verification results and decisions taken are in [`../06-niche-research-review.md`](../06-niche-research-review.md).

## Headline recommendation
Launch with **backup water systems** (tanks, pumps, booster sets, refills). Runner-up: **emergency plumbing and geysers** (steadiest demand, most competition).

## Demand evidence claimed
- Rand Water (23 Oct 2026): Johannesburg exceeded its temporary water-use target every month Jan–Sep 2026; September 1,724 ML/day vs 1,528 target (via Mail & Guardian).
- Johannesburg Water (29 Oct 2026): systems "severely constrained", naming Bryanston, Illovo and Morningside (Sandton system) and Honeydew, Cosmo City and Waterval Tower (Randburg–Roodepoort); pressure throttling; proposed Level 1 restrictions (~15% demand cut); Level 2 possible.
- By 2 Nov 2026: Sandton, Midrand, Randburg, Roodepoort, Commando, Central and Soweto systems in their second week of outages and low pressure.
- Jack Bloom (27 Oct 2026): non-revenue water ~49% (physical losses 26%).
- Rand Water maintenance 29 May–19 Jul 2026 (phase one hit Sandton); Dec 2025/Jan 2026 holiday shutdowns, so another December shutdown is plausible but unconfirmed.
- Electricity: Eskom said on 16 May 2026 it had gone a full year without load-shedding; Gauteng "load reduction" continues; City Power 8-hour maintenance outages and Northriding substation faults (Apr and Oct 2026).
- Property market: Jan 2026 Homes of Distinction note says backup water, boreholes and inverters speed up sales; Bryanston listings advertise "Backup Water" as a headline feature.
- No Google Trends / Keyword Planner data. Demand inferred from news, contractor SEO pages and Marketplace listings.

## Pricing benchmarks claimed
- Snupit: R30/credit (R24 bulk), 1–5 credits per shared lead (~R24–R150); refund if no win from first 50 credits; 30,000 requests/month.
- Bark: ~US$2.20–2.35/credit (US figures), leads shared with up to 5 pros.
- Kandua (Santam): June 2026 move from pay-per-lead to one-to-one matching with AI triage.
- Suggested pricing (author's estimate): R300–R500 shared (max 3), **R600–R900 exclusive phone-verified install lead**, R100–R200 refill lead; area retainer R4k–R8k/month.
- Claim: "At a 25–35% close rate on R16,000 jobs, a R700 exclusive lead is still under 15% of a typical installer's gross margin per job."
- JoJo price rise of 31% from 1 Apr 2026 (force majeure; LLDPE +50%), per BusinessTech 15 Apr 2026.

## Week-one plan (as received)
Domain + suburb pages (Sandton, Bryanston, Randburg, Morningside, Illovo, Ferndale/Blairgowrie, Northriding); live outage status page; 2026 price table; litres calculator; tracked numbers + 3-step form with OTP; recruit 3–5 installers (first 5 leads free, then R400–R700); expansion: plumbing/geysers (wk 3–4), gate motors (month 2), boreholes (upsell).

## Compliance notes (as received)
POPIA s18 notice at collection; Dec 2024 Guidance Note on Direct Marketing (sharing leads = further processing; phone calls treated as electronic communication under s69); fines up to R10m; CPA national opt-out registry (direct-marketer registration 15 Sep–31 Dec 2026, consumer blocking from May 2027); RICA s4/s5/s6 for call recording; PIRB CoC for geysers; registered electricians and COCs (Electrical Installation Regulations 2009, regs 6, 7(4), 7(5), 9(1)); boreholes: no national registration for Schedule 1 domestic use (DWS to Outa, Jul 2026) but City of Johannesburg registration (~R1,185) and drilling approval.

## Caveats stated by the author
No suburb search-volume data; contractor price pages may anchor high; geyser prices conflict; per-lead and retainer prices are estimates; the crisis may ease, but structural overuse and 49% non-revenue water suggest recurring demand.
